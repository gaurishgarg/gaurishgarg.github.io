Traditional Indian Games in Mixed Reality

User Profile

" As a 12 yeare old, I lieke to play video games a lot. However, I would like to know about traditional indian games, as I have heard from my grandmother about them a lot, but I want an interactive element in that"

Problem Statement

  • With the rise of mobile and PC games, emerging generation does not know much about traditional Indian GameS
  • Lack of interactive digital elements in traditional Games that can fasicinate the emerging generation.

Design Challenge

Design an immersive Mixed Reality experience that introduces traditional Indian games to the emerging generation while maintaining cultural authenticity and providing engaging interactive elements comparable to modern digital games.

Target Platform

Goal Statement

Experience traditional Indian Games in Mixed Reality boosting Interactive Play

Scope

Connecting Indian Culture to Mixed Reality

Concept

Users interact with virtual representations of traditional Indian games through hand-based interaction in a Mixed Reality environment, enabling exploratory play and learning through embodied interaction rather than screen-based controls.
