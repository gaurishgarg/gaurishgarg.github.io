Hi! I'm Gaurish

I am a PhD student at the Centre for Educational Technology, IIT Bombay. My focus lies in tech-informed tangible learning environments, where AR, VR and physical artefacts come together so that computational ideas are not only seen on a screen but also felt and acted upon. Having completed my Master of Technology in Augmented Reality and Virtual Reality from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Jodhpur 🥈 (Institute Silver Medalist), I continue to work at the intersection of design and engineering, bridging human-centred design and engineering principles so that these learning systems are not only user-friendly but also technically feasible on end systems.
